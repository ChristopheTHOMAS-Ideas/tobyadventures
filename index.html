About the series

One little dog.
A world of discovery.

Toby’s Adventures is a collection of coloring and activity books for little explorers ages 3–5. With his backpack and sunglasses, Toby travels to new destinations, inviting children to color, learn, play, and explore along the way.

Each adventure brings a new place to discover, with simple activities inspired by its landscapes, animals, culture, and everyday life. First stop: Morocco. Next stop: Paris!

Small dog. Big adventures.

Curious? Start here!

Who is Toby?

Toby is a curious little black French Bulldog with big upright ears, round sunglasses, and a brown-orange travel backpack. He’s the friendly explorer at the heart of every Toby’s Adventures book.

What is the Toby’s Adventures series about?

Small dog. Big adventures! Each book takes Toby to a different destination and invites children to discover its places, animals, culture, and everyday life through coloring and simple activities.

What ages are Toby’s coloring and activity books for?

Toby’s Adventures is designed for kids ages 3–5. The simple coloring, tracing, drawing, counting, and matching activities are made for preschoolers, with space for little hands to explore.

What’s inside the Morocco activity book?

Toby’s Adventures in Morocco is an 80-page paperback coloring and activity book. Children can color Moroccan scenes, trace and draw, try easy mazes, practice shapes and counting, and discover the country’s landscapes, animals, and culture.

Are the books in English?

Yes. Toby’s Adventures in Morocco is an English-language book. The Amazon country links above let you choose a local store; they all refer to the same English-language edition.

Where can I buy Toby’s Adventures?

The Morocco book is available on Amazon. Choose your Amazon store to view the book. Toby’s Adventures in Paris is coming soon.

What changes from one adventure to the next?

A new destination brings new scenes, discoveries, and a new travel look for Toby! In Morocco, he wears a red fez. In Paris, he swaps it for a French beret and a striped outfit. His big ears, sunglasses, backpack, and curious personality stay with him.

Do we need to follow the books in order?

Each book focuses on its own destination, so you can explore the places that interest your child as the collection grows. Morocco is Toby’s first published adventure and is available now.

Where is Toby going next?

Next stop: Paris! Toby’s Adventures in Paris is coming soon, with new places to discover and more coloring and activities. More destinations will follow. Release dates haven’t been announced yet.
